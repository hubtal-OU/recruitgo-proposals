Service Process

This section outlines the one-time onboarding steps and the recurring monthly payroll and compliance cycle. Click each tab to see the details.

Onboarding Flow

  1. 1

    Contract Finalization (2 Days)

    RecruitGo prepares the locally compliant employment contract in Spanish.

  2. 2

    Employee Documentation (1-2 Days)

    Employee submits required documents (DNI, CUIT/CUIL, Bank Info).

  3. 3

    Statutory Registration

    RecruitGo registers the employee with AFIP (Tax) and SIPA (Social Security).

  4. ✓

    Ready to Work

    Total estimated time: 7-10 business days.

Monthly Payroll & Compliance Cycle

  1. 1

    Integra Input (by 5th of Month)

    Integra submits any variable pay (bonuses, overtime) via the **RecruitGo Web Application**.

  2. 2

    Invoice (by 15th of Month)

    RecruitGo issues the single monthly invoice to Integra (Payment Terms: Net 7).

  3. 3

    Payroll Calculation & Filing

    RecruitGo calculates net pay, withholdings, and files all statutory declarations (Declaración Jurada) with AFIP.

  4. 4

    Payment & Reporting (by End of Month)

    RecruitGo pays the employee's net salary, remits all taxes, and provides a full payroll report to Integra.

Benefits & Legal

Explore the mandatory legal entitlements and benefits your employee will receive under Argentine law, all managed by RecruitGo.

Mandatory Employment Conditions

  • Probation Period:90 days (3 months)
  • Annual Leave:14 calendar days (for < 5 years service)
  • Sick Leave:3 months paid leave per year (doubled to 6 if employee has dependents)
  • Maternity Leave:90 calendar days (paid via Social Security)
  • Paternity Leave:2 consecutive paid days
  • Marriage Leave:10 consecutive days
  • Notice Period (by Employer):1 month (0-5 years service) or 2 months (>5 years)
  • Severance (w/o cause):1 month's highest regular salary per year of service

Mandatory Statutory Benefits

(Included in employer/employee contributions)

  • Health Insurance (Obras Sociales): Access to mandatory basic healthcare.
  • Pension Fund (SIPA): Enrollment in the national retirement system.
  • Labour Risk Insurance (ART): Coverage for work-related injuries.

Optional / Supplemental Benefits

(Available upon request, cost added to invoice)

  • Private Health Insurance (Prepaga): We can administer enrollment in supplemental private health plans, which are a highly valued benefit in Argentina.

Handling Payroll Administration & Adjustments

  • Allowances:Handled as non-salary compensation where legally permissible or grossed up as taxable income.
  • Bonuses:Treated as taxable compensation and subject to all social security contributions. Included in SAC calculation.
  • Overtime:Processed at 50% (weekdays) or 100% (weekends/holidays) premium, as submitted monthly by Integra.
  • Statutory Adjustments:We automatically monitor and apply all changes to minimum wage, tax brackets, and contribution caps.

Terms & Data

This final section covers important details regarding financial terms, data security, and your dedicated point of contact at RecruitGo.

Financial & Billing Terms

  • Billing Cycle:Monthly. Invoice issued on the 15th for the current month.
  • Payment Terms:Net 7 days. Payment must be received within 7 calendar days.
  • Invoice Contents:Yes, all employer taxes and contributions are included in the $3,000 TME portion of the invoice.
  • Currency:The $3,000 TME is a USD nominal anchor. Payroll is calculated and paid in ARS based on the official exchange rate, ensuring compliance.
